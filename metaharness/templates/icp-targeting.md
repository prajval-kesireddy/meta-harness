## Definition of done

- ICP.md exists and is falsifiable: firmographics, buying trigger, pain in the buyer's own mined words, and DISQUALIFIERS (who looks right but isn't).
- 2-4 segments scored on fit and reachability, with one declared primary.
- A named list per segment at the configured scale, spot-verified (1 in 10 entries checked against the profile).
- Per-segment messaging kit in the buyer's language, de-slopped.
- TARGETING-BRIEF.md: the test that settles the hypothesis, and the 3 assumptions only real outreach can answer.

## Phases

### Phase 0: Evidence mining (fresh session)
Paste:
> Read HARNESS.md and PROCESS-CORE.md. Mine before inventing. With customers: profile the best ones (why did they buy, what triggered the search, what words do they use for the pain; pull from calls, emails, reviews). Without: build the problem-intensity case (who feels this pain most acutely, most often, with budget) and pressure-test it against community evidence: find real people complaining about this problem in public and capture their language verbatim. Everything lands in EVIDENCE.md tagged evidence or assumption.

### Phase 1: The profile and segments
Paste:
> From EVIDENCE.md write ICP.md: firmographics/demographics, the trigger event, the pain in THEIR words, willingness-to-pay signal, and the disqualifier list. Then cut 2-4 segments and score each on pain intensity, reachability, and evidence strength. Declare a primary and a challenger.

USER GATE: approve ICP.md. Read the disqualifiers hardest; lists built on a loose profile waste the whole downstream budget.

### Phase 2: List building
Paste:
> Build the named list per segment at the configured scale using the free-tier stack: search-only discovery for names and accounts, cheap enrichment for contact data, dedup, then score every entry against ICP.md. Never spend paid credits where a free tier does the job. Spot-verify 1 in 10 entries by actually looking at them; a list that doesn't match the profile is worse than no list.

### Phase 3: Message-market fit
Paste:
> Per segment, write the messaging kit in the buyer's mined language: the pain named the way THEY name it, one proof point, one clear ask. Then the de-slop loop, ruthlessly; AI-sounding outreach gets deleted on sight by exactly the buyers worth reaching. Output per configured motion: sequences (outbound), content angles (inbound), or a seller's targeting brief (sales).

### Phase 4: The test kit
Paste:
> Write TARGETING-BRIEF.md: which segment gets tested first, with what sample size, and what reply/conversion rate confirms or kills the hypothesis; plus the 3 assumptions in ICP.md that only real contact can settle, each mapped to what outcome would settle it. Verify every list file and kit exists per PROCESS-CORE.md before reporting done.
