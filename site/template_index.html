Process engineering, done for you.

The people getting stunning output engineered a working layer by hand: staged pipelines, iteration loops with exit conditions, verification gates that demand evidence. metaharness builds that machine around your outcome in one command.

01 — the interview

Say what you're making

Eight outcomes, a stated three or four questions each, up front. Follow-up questions come later, at gates where you have something to react to: pick an aesthetic after the research, approve the script before the render.

02 — the composition

The harness gets built

Minimal skill installs (more makes agents worse), a staged pipeline with the model named per stage, runbook prompts, and the usage budget written down before any work starts.

03 — the loops

Loops until it's actually good

Design loops audit real screenshots. Research loops run until two dry rounds. Verification gates demand the render, the live URL, the evidence, before anything gets called done.
