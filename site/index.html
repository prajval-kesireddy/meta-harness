Give your AI the intuition of the best in the world at any task.

A layer sits between your prompt and great output: the harness. Which skills are installed, how the work loops, what gets verified before the agent says done. The stunning results on your feed come from people who engineered that layer by hand.

metaharness asks a few questions about the outcome you want, then composes that layer for you from a rated index of the whole ecosystem. The token bill is stated before you start.

Eight outcomes, each with its own harness.

A harness here means the full working layer: the two or three skills genuinely worth installing (more makes agents worse), a phase-by-phase runbook with paste-ready prompts, iteration loops with written exit conditions, and verification gates that demand evidence before "done."

The bill, up front.

Harness runs burn real plan usage, and pretending otherwise is how people end up rate-limited mid-project. Every composed harness states its estimate as a band. A single-page website with full design loops, for example:

your planshare of weekly usage
Claude Pro~3–16%
Claude Max 5x~1–4%
Claude Max 20x~1–3%

Bands assume a Sonnet-heavy mix; Opus-heavy runs burn roughly five times faster. Media generation (video and image models) bills separately and every estimate says so where it applies.
